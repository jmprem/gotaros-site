About

Runtime monitoring and oversight for autonomous systems.

TAROS, the Trusted Autonomous Runtime Oversight System, is built by jamberi to monitor autonomous systems and AI/SI (Artificial Intelligence or Super Intelligence) agents while they run—so organizations can deploy them with evidence rather than hope.

We believe that as AI/SI becomes more autonomous and embedded in critical workflows, enterprises need continuous runtime monitoring, enforceable policies, and a trustworthy record of every decision.

Our mission is to provide that foundation—so that platform teams, governance leads, and decision-makers can deploy AI/SI with greater confidence and clearer oversight.
